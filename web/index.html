The core promise

Missed calls should not become lost context.

Call Held turns calls you miss or decline into organized outcomes: summaries, caller requests, decisions, topic updates, and follow-ups. It protects attention without pretending your phone never rings.

More than a message taker

A conversational assistant with context.

Call Held does not just record missed calls. Your assistant can ask follow-up questions, understand caller intent, and capture the outcome so you do not have to reconstruct the call later.

01

Talks with callers

The assistant can have a natural conversation with missed or declined callers to understand what they need and whether the request is urgent.

02

Recognizes known people

When you allow contact access, Call Held can recognize known callers and use relationship context to handle family, trusted contacts, and unknown callers differently.

03

Remembers prior calls

For repeat callers, the assistant can use previous summaries, notes, and topic context so every conversation does not have to start from zero.

04

Helps with scheduling

When calendar access is connected, the assistant can check availability, answer scheduling questions, and create meetings or reminders when appropriate.

How it works

You ring first. Your assistant catches what you miss.

01

Phone rings normally

Keep your existing mobile number. Incoming calls still ring on your phone first.

02

Missed calls forward

If you do not answer, or you tap the red decline button, the call forwards to your assistant.

03

Intent gets captured

The assistant asks who is calling, why they called, and whether the request is urgent.

04

You get the outcome

You see caller identity, urgency, notes, summaries, and suggested follow-ups in the app.

Built for real life

Calls become memory, not clutter.

The assistant learns caller context, keeps useful notes separate from raw transcripts, and groups communication into topic-aware situations over time.

  • Caller recognition for known contacts
  • Call summaries and follow-up actions
  • Topic threads for ongoing situations
  • Live answer and transfer requests
