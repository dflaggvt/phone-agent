Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Request Account Deletion

Call Held users can request deletion of their account and associated app data at any time. If you can access the app, open Profile and choose Remove account. If you cannot access the app, email support@callheld.com from the email address associated with your Call Held account.

What To Include

To help us locate the correct account, include:

  • The email address used for your Call Held account.
  • The mobile number protected by your assistant, if one was verified.
  • Whether you want to delete the full account or only specific data categories.

Partial Data Deletion

You may request deletion of specific data without deleting your full account. Examples include call history, transcripts, summaries, topics, assistant notes, synced contacts, calendar connection data, notification history, or support records. Email support@callheld.com and describe the data you want deleted.

Data Deleted

When an account deletion request is verified, we delete or de-identify user-owned app data, including:

  • Account profile, assistant settings, onboarding state, and phone-routing configuration.
  • Call records, transcripts, summaries, caller memory, topic memory, tasks, decisions, and notes.
  • Synced contact records and connected-calendar tokens or activity controlled by Call Held.
  • Push notification tokens and local app access associated with the account.

Data We May Retain

We may retain limited records when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or compliance purposes. Examples include payment and invoice records handled by our payment provider, security logs, audit records, and deletion-request records. Retained records are limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Timing

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 7 days and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days unless a longer period is required by law, security review, billing dispute, or technical backup retention. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before automatic expiration.

Contact

Questions about account or data deletion can be sent to support@callheld.com.
